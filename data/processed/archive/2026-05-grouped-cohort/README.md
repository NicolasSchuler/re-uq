# Archived: May 2026 metric snapshots

Compact metric snapshots written by the notebook path during the original
submission's campaign (run group `provider-matrix-2026-05`: six models, 16
items per request). The revised manuscript's numbers are generated from
`outputs/paper_*.csv` (run group `manuscript-final`); the per-cell metrics of
that campaign are written to `outputs/evaluation_<cell>_<run_id>/` by
`scripts/generate_evaluation_analysis.py` and shipped in the Zenodo dataset
record rather than tracked here.

| `metrics_summary_preliminary.csv` | The preliminary pass that preceded the full cohort |
