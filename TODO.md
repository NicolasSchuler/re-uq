## Out of scope: fine-tuning

The study measures off-the-shelf behaviour of instruction-tuned models under
a frozen prompt contract. Whether fine-tuning removes modal-force
strengthening is a different paper; it is recorded as a limitation in
[`docs/experimental_setup.md`](docs/experimental_setup.md), not as planned work.
